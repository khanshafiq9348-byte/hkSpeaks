# Voice Cloning Safeguards & Policies

## Safeguards & Legal Authorization
Voice cloning involves sensitive biometric and personal rights. HK Speaks enforces the following controls:
1. **Explicit Legal Consent**: The user must explicitly check the confirmation box declaring they own the voice or hold written authorization.
2. **Acceptable Use Policy Agreement**: Agreement not to use the voice clone for deceptive impersonation, fraud, or defamatory purposes.
3. **Private by Default**: All cloned voice models are created as `is_public: false` and attached to the user's specific account ID.
4. **File Validation**:
   - MIME validation and extension checks
   - Minimum duration of 3 seconds
   - Maximum duration of 300 seconds
   - Max file size of 25MB
5. **Instant Deletion**: Users can delete their custom voice clones at any time, removing audio source keys and deactivating the voice model.
